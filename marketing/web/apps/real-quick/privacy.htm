Real Quick — Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 October 2026 · App by Karol Krakowski

The short version

Real Quick has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers. Your food diary, goal and settings stay on your phone. To count calories, a food photo (or a meal you typed) is sent to our server and on to an AI model, which returns the estimate. We do not keep the photo.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Food photos you snap, shrunk to about 1000 pixels, and typed meals the app cannot recognise on its own. They are sent over HTTPS to our gateway (realquick.lab.karolkrakowski.pl, a server run by us), which passes them to our AI provider and returns calories and macros. The gateway does not store the photo or the text.
  • A random install ID created by the app on first launch, and your IP address, used only to count how many free AI counts were used today (3 per day). These counters are deleted after two days.
  • If you subscribe to Pro: your App Store / Google Play subscription receipt, so the gateway can confirm the subscription. We keep only a daily usage counter for it.

Processors

AI analysis is performed by Anthropic, PBC (Claude) — or, if we switch providers, by Perplexity AI, Inc.; this page will name the one in use. The provider processes the photo or text only to produce the estimate and may keep API inputs for a short period for abuse monitoring under its own terms; under its commercial terms it does not train models on them. Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

What stays on your phone

Your food diary and photo thumbnails, your daily goal and the optional body details used to suggest it (sex, age, height, weight, activity), and all settings. Widgets read a small summary of today from your phone, not from the internet.

Camera

Real Quick asks for camera access to show the live preview next to the snap button and to take food photos. The camera only runs while the app is open on screen. The app never records audio or video.

Live sharing

Sharing is off until you turn it on. When you start sharing, the app sends a summary of your days — daily calorie and macro totals, the names and calories of the meals you counted, and the name you type for yourself — to our gateway, which shows it on a page at a random, unguessable link that only people you send it to can open. It stays current while sharing is on and is deleted from our server the moment you tap "Stop sharing" (or erase your data). Photos are never part of it.

Deleting your data

Settings ▸ "Erase all my data" deletes everything on the phone and stops any live share; deleting the app does the same for the phone. On our side there is nothing else beyond the anonymous daily counters (which expire within two days), the day your install first counted (kept so the free days cannot be reset by reinstalling), and a live share for as long as you keep it on.

Children

Real Quick is not directed at children under 13 and is not intended for anyone with an eating disorder.

Health

Calorie and macro numbers are estimates for everyday tracking, not medical advice.

karol@karolkrakowski.pl — we reply within 2 business days.

Real Quick プライバシーポリシー

最終更新：2026年10月26日

Real Quick にはアカウント、広告、分析ツール、トラッカーはありません。食事の記録・目標・設定はお使いの端末内にのみ保存されます。カロリーを計算するため、撮影した食事の写真（または入力した食事）を当社サーバー経由でAIモデルに送信し、推定値を受け取ります。写真は保存しません。

端末から送信されるもの

  • 食事の写真（約1000ピクセルに縮小）と、端末内で判別できなかった入力した食事。HTTPSで当社ゲートウェイに送られ、AIプロバイダー（Anthropic, PBC）で解析されます。ゲートウェイは写真や文章を保存しません。
  • 無料回数（1日3回）を数えるためのランダムなインストールIDとIPアドレス。2日以内に削除されます。
  • Pro をご利用の場合、購読を確認するためのApp Store / Google Play のレシート。

カメラ

撮影ボタン横のプレビューと食事の撮影のためにのみ使用し、アプリが画面に表示されている間だけ動作します。音声や動画は記録しません。

データの削除

「設定」▸「すべてのデータを消去」、またはアプリの削除で端末内のデータはすべて消えます。
