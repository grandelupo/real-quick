FAQ

How do I snap from the Lock Screen?
Press and hold the Lock Screen ▸ Customize ▸ Lock Screen ▸ tap the widget row ▸ Real Quick ▸ "Snap". On iOS 18 you can also replace the flashlight or camera button with the "Snap food" control. On Android, add the 1×1 "Snap" widget to your home screen.

Why does my iPhone ask me to unlock first?
iOS only lets apps use the camera after Face ID / Touch ID. Look at your phone, tap Snap, and the photo is taken as soon as it unlocks.

Why does the app stay open after a widget snap on iPhone?
iOS does not allow apps to close themselves. On Android, Real Quick closes again by itself.

The numbers look off.
Swipe left, tap the entry, and pick a portion (½ to 2×) or type the calories you know. You can also make all future estimates lean lighter or heavier in Settings ▸ Estimates.

A snap says "Counting…" for a long time.
You are probably offline. Snaps are saved and counted automatically when you are back online.

What does the free version include?
3 AI counts every day, plus unlimited typed common foods ("banana", "2 eggs and toast"). Real Quick Pro removes the daily limit.

How do I cancel Pro?
iPhone: Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions ▸ Real Quick. Android: Play Store ▸ Profile ▸ Payments & subscriptions. Or Real Quick ▸ Settings ▸ Manage subscription.

How do I delete my data?
Real Quick ▸ Settings ▸ "Erase all my data", or delete the app. There is no account, so there is nothing else to delete.

Is this medical advice?
No. Real Quick shows estimates for everyday tracking. If you have a medical condition or an eating disorder, please follow your clinician's guidance.
