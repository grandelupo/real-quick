Real Quick – The Fastest Calorie Tracker.

Tap one button. Real Quick snaps your plate and counts the calories, protein, carbs and fat while you eat. No searching databases, no weighing, no typing.

HOW IT WORKS
• One big button: tap it and forget it. A live mini camera sits right next to it, so the photo is taken instantly.
• Today’s calories and macros, front and centre, against your daily goal — and a small 3D forest below that grows with what you eat, tilts with your phone, and turns when you overdo it.
• Swipe up and down to visit other days. Every weekday has its own soft colour and object; after 8 pm everything turns into a calm night sheet.
• Swipe left for the food list: fix a portion, remove something, or just type “2 eggs and toast”.
• Share a live link with your partner: they see today and your history for as long as you keep sharing.

SNAP FROM THE HOME SCREEN
Add the 1×1 Snap widget to your home screen. One tap opens the camera, snaps, confirms and closes again — that’s the whole interaction.

ESTIMATES YOUR WAY
Photos can’t see the butter in the pan. Choose “Go easy”, “Straight up” or “Keep me honest” and Real Quick leans its estimates the way you prefer.

SIMPLE ON PURPOSE
Real Quick does one thing: it counts what you eat in the time it takes to take a photo. No accounts, no feeds, no meal plans.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account. Your food diary stays on your phone. A photo is sent only to count it and is not kept on our servers.

REAL QUICK PRO
The first 3 days are free, every feature included. After that, Real Quick Pro counts every photo, typed meal and widget snap — each count runs a real vision model, which is what the subscription pays for.
